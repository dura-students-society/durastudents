About the Society

Connecting Dura youth with their heritage and future.

Dura Students Society is a youth-focused platform for connection, learning, cultural documentation and community engagement within the Dura Indigenous Community.

Our work recognizes that heritage is not only found in monuments and written records. It also lives in language, memories, stories, songs, places, photographs, family histories, traditional knowledge and the voices of elders.

We therefore work toward a future where Dura youth can discover where they come from, understand their identity, learn their language and actively contribute to preserving the knowledge of their community.

Dura Digital Heritage Project

Document.

The Dura Digital Heritage Project is a proposed community-owned digital heritage ecosystem designed to systematically document, digitize, preserve and share Dura history, language, culture, oral traditions, people and places.

The project is designed as a long-term community asset rather than simply a website.

Youth & Community

The next generation should not inherit a gap in memory.

Dura youth have an important role in documenting, learning, digitizing and sharing community knowledge. The project is designed to create practical opportunities for youth volunteers to participate in field documentation, archiving, language work, technology, media and outreach.

Community Governance

Preserving heritage also means protecting cultural rights.

Community ownership

The archive is intended to remain a community asset beyond individual projects and contributors.

Consent

Heritage materials should enter the system with documented contributor consent.

Cultural sensitivity

Sensitive cultural knowledge can be managed through Public, Community, Restricted and Archival access levels.

Verification

Records can carry transparent statuses such as Verified, Community Account, Research Interpretation and Unknown.
