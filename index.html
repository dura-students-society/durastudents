Why we exist

Dura heritage lives in language, memories, places, people, photographs, documents, songs, stories, rituals, and everyday knowledge.

The Dura Digital Heritage Project is designed to systematically document, digitize, archive, and share the Dura community's language, history, cultural practices, oral traditions, traditional knowledge, places, people, and material culture.

“The archive should remain a community asset even when individual project members change.”

Project pathway

Start carefully. Build sustainably. Grow with the community.

PHASE I · MONTHS 1–6

Foundation

Governance, heritage taxonomy, metadata, database design, archival policy, and a pilot collection.

PHASE II · MONTHS 6–18

Documentation

Field documentation of oral histories, language, places, people, and cultural practices.

PHASE III · MONTHS 12–24

Digitization

Systematic conversion of photographs, documents, audio, and video into preserved digital masters.

PHASE IV · MONTHS 18–30

Platform Development

Public platform with search, maps, galleries, timeline, multilingual interface, and content management.

PHASE V · MONTHS 28–34

Public Launch

Security and consent audits, content thresholds, integrations, public rollout, and community training.

PHASE VI · ONGOING

Long-Term Development

Future enhancements such as transcription, OCR, semantic search, knowledge graphs, and virtual experiences.

Community governance

Built with the Dura community, not merely about it.

The proposed governance structure distributes responsibility across documentation, archives, language, cultural verification, technology, media, and youth outreach so the work does not depend on one person or organization.
